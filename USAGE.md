# Quick Usage Guide

## Getting Started in 4 Steps

### 1️⃣ Open a Conversation
Navigate to ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, or DeepSeek and open any conversation.

### 2️⃣ Find the Export Button
- **ChatGPT/Grok/Gemini**: Look for the purple gradient button in the header (download arrow icon)
- **DeepSeek**: Look for the floating button on the right side
- The button appears automatically when the page loads

### 3️⃣ Enable Selection & Select Messages
- Click the export button to enable selection mode
- Checkboxes will appear next to each message
- Click messages to select/deselect them (they'll turn green)
- A banner shows your selection count

### 4️⃣ Export
- Click the export button again to open the popup
- Choose your format (Markdown, Word, or PDF)
- Click **"Export Selected"** to download

---

## Selection Tips

### Individual Selection
- Click any message to toggle its selection
- Selected messages turn green with a checkmark
- Click again to deselect

### Bulk Selection
- **Select All** - Selects every message in the conversation
- **Clear All** - Deselects all messages

### Export Without Selection
If you don't select any messages and click Export, you'll be prompted to export the entire conversation.

## Troubleshooting

### Button Not Appearing
- Wait a moment for the page to fully load
- The button automatically appears after 1 second
- When switching conversations, it recreates automatically
- If still not visible, try refreshing the page

### Selection Mode Not Working
- Make sure you're on a supported platform (ChatGPT, Grok, or DeepSeek)
- Refresh the page and try again
- Check browser console for error messages

### Export Button Disabled
- Enable Selection Mode first
- Make sure you're on a conversation page (not the home page)

### Downloaded File Won't Open
- **Markdown** - Open with any text editor, VS Code, or note-taking app
- **Word** - Open with Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or any browser
- **PDF** - Use "Print to PDF" in your browser if direct PDF export doesn't work

### No Messages Detected
- Scroll through the conversation first to load all messages
- Some platforms may have updated their UI - check for extension updates
